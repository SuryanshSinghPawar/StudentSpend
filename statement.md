# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Students often make small daily expenses without maintaining a clear record of where their money is going. This makes it difficult to compare spending with a monthly budget and identify high-spending categories.

## Scope
StudentSpend provides a simple command-line system for recording expenses, setting monthly budgets, and generating spending summaries. The project focuses on personal student expense tracking and local data storage.

## Target Users
- College students
- Students living in hostels or PGs
- Students who want a simple personal budget tracker

## High-Level Features
1. Expense management
2. Monthly budget management
3. Spending analytics and reports
4. Local JSON data persistence
5. Input validation and testing
